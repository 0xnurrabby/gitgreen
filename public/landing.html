FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this safe for my GitHub account?

Yes. GitGreen uses a scoped token with repo access, never your password. You can pause or remove any account in one click.

How is the free trial different?

You get a full 30-day trial with one account and the same autopilot as paid plans. No credit card required.

How do I pay?

With crypto, including BTC, ETH, USDT and 300+ coins. Upgrades only charge the difference and keep your expiry date.

Can I pause anytime?

Yes. Pause your autopilot instantly from the dashboard. Your accounts simply pause until you resume.
