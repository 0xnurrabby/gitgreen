About IconFinder

Searches and downloads icons from a local SVG library.

Why this exists

The project started as a small tool to solve one specific problem. Over time it grew into a dependable utility that stays focused and easy to extend. The goal is simple: do the core job well and get out of the way.

Design principles

  • Small core, rich ecosystem.
  • Plain data over clever abstractions.
  • Clear errors over silent failures.
  • Fast by default, tunable when needed.
IconFinder - built with plain HTML, CSS and Node.
